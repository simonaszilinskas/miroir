# T6: writing the 50 hand-made word searches

T6 (technical spec §7) is 50 French searches written by people, the way a visitor would type them. T7 is the same idea written by a model; T6 checks that T7 tells the truth.

## How to write one

1. Open a random record on POP (Joconde, Mémoire or Rose Valland), look at its main image for ten seconds, then close the record. Do not read the title or the description first.
2. Write what you would type to find it again, as if you were standing in front of it: "fontaine en pierre avec un lion", "portrait d'homme en noir avec une fraise", "moulin à vent en Vendée".
3. Mix the lengths: about half a few words, half one sentence. Write some with everyday words and some with the precise ones ("tympan", "faïence", "retable").
4. Use a name only if a visitor would know it without the label (Pont du Gard, Mont-Saint-Michel). Never copy the title, an inventory number or a caption.
5. Five of the 50 may be in English, five may hold a typo you would really make.

## What to record

One line per search in a shared sheet: the search, the record's POP reference (the right answer), your initials. Aim for 20 Mémoire buildings, 20 Joconde objects, 10 Rose Valland works.

## Judging

A search scores when the right record is in the top 10. Some searches will find another record that fits just as well (another windmill in Vendée); a second person marks those "also right" by hand, so T6 counts what a visitor would accept. Keep half the lines for the sealed half, chosen by coin toss, and do not look at their scores until round R4.
